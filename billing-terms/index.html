Subscription billing

Smarti Plus is billed through your Apple ID at the price shown on the paywall at the moment of purchase. Your subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Prices may vary by region and are shown in your local currency on the paywall and in your App Store receipt.

Trials

If a free trial is offered, it begins at purchase and converts to a paid subscription at the end of the trial period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You can cancel during the trial from Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions without being charged.

Managing and cancelling

Manage or cancel your subscription from Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your device. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep Smarti Plus access until that period ends, then the subscription stops renewing.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly, but we're happy to help you file a request — write to vladaverti99@gmail.com.
