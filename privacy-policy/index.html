Information We Collect

Smarti is a remote control for LG webOS televisions on your local Wi-Fi network. To discover and connect to a TV we read local network information such as the TV's IP address, its name on the network, and the pairing key the TV issues on first connection. Pairing keys are stored on your device in the iOS Keychain.

We may also receive subscription status, a device identifier, crash logs, and basic usage analytics to deliver and support Smarti Plus.

How We Use Information

We use your information to discover and control your TV, deliver Smarti features, manage Smarti Plus subscriptions, troubleshoot issues, and improve product quality.

We may use aggregated or de-identified data for analytics, research, and service improvement.

Sharing

We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the app, such as Apple (for App Store payments and subscription validation), Adapty (for subscription management and entitlement checks), and error-monitoring and analytics providers.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

Your Choices

You can stop using Smarti at any time, remove paired TVs from the app's settings, manage your Smarti Plus subscription through your Apple ID in Settings › Subscriptions, and contact support regarding privacy-related questions.

Where required by law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information.
