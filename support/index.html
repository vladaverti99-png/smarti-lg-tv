My TV isn't found

Smarti controls LG webOS televisions over your local Wi-Fi. Make sure the phone and the TV are on the same network (not a guest network or VLAN), and that the TV is turned on — not in standby.

In the TV: Settings › General › Network — check the TV is connected. In the iPhone: give Smarti the Local Network permission when prompted. If you missed it, enable it under iOS Settings › Smarti › Local Network.

I get a pairing prompt on the TV every launch

The first connection asks the TV to approve the remote. After you confirm on-screen, Smarti stores a pairing key in your iPhone's Keychain so the next launch is silent.

If the prompt keeps coming back, the TV lost the saved client (common after a factory reset or a firmware update). Approve once more, then Smarti will remember the TV again.

Voice control isn't listening

Voice input needs microphone and speech-recognition access. Grant both when iOS asks, or enable them later under iOS Settings › Smarti › Microphone and Speech Recognition.

Recognition runs on-device by iOS. Your voice isn't sent to Smarti's servers.

Billing and subscription questions

Smarti Plus is billed through your Apple ID. Manage, pause or cancel any time under iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling before the end of the current period keeps Plus active until that period ends, then stops renewals.

Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly, but we're happy to help you file one.
